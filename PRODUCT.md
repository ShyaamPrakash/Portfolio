## Evidence on Hand
plan.md contains supplied resume facts and visual-reference descriptions. Original screenshots, PDF, project media, Supabase configuration and a licensed Rive asset are absent. No unverified professional experience, proficiency, public email or availability claims may be added.

## Brand Commitments
The supplied inspiration establishes two visual worlds: electric-blue technical aircraft drawings, then pale ground with dark particle studies. The new interface uses both as animated ASCII scenes, with tabs that replace the main exhibit and its related information in place. User requests anticipation, follow-through, eased arcs, tactile feedback, deliberate staging, large targets, familiar controls and less animation for frequent actions. Tab transitions stay below 300ms; native page scroll remains available without smooth-scroll override.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard and touch navigation, readable contrast, reduced-motion equivalents and a static WebGL fallback are required. Content remains visible before animation initializes.

## Open Decisions
Owner must provide deployment/auth configuration, approve initial published projects and supply or approve a valid Rive asset. Optional personal links and additional skill claims remain unpublished.
